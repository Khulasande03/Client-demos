About us

Small team,
outsized impact.

Founded in 2016, Pixel & Co is a tight-knit team of designers, developers, and strategists who believe great design is never decoration — it's the engine of business growth.

We've partnered with startups and Fortune 500s alike, delivering work that wins awards and, more importantly, wins customers. We're selective with who we work with so we can go deep — not wide.

  • Strategy first. We spend time understanding your business before opening Figma.
  • No juniors on your project. You work directly with senior designers and developers.
  • On-time, always. We've shipped every project on time in 8 years. No exceptions.
